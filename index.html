One day the cities we call home may sink quietly beneath the rising sea.

Since 1993, sea levels have risen about 11 cm, and the rate has nearly doubled as warming seawater expands and ice melts.

Source: WMO – State of the Global Climate 2025; Eos – Sea level rise is accelerating

Burning fossil fuels has pushed greenhouse gases to their highest levels in 800,000 years, and Greenland and Antarctica are now losing ice 5–6 times faster than 30 years ago.

Source: WMO – State of the Global Climate 2025; ICCI State of the Cryosphere 2025 (summary)

Closer than you think

The sea is not rising somewhere far away but in the cities we grew up in

  • Ho Chi Minh City: The city has sunk an estimated 1 metre since 1990, and high tides in late 2025 broke historical records. (VTC News)
  • Cà Mau: The province loses over 400 hectares of land to the sea every year. (Nhân Dân)
  • Mekong Delta: Nearly 1.7 million people have left over the past decade, as droughts, saltwater intrusion and floods cut farm incomes. (Tuổi Trẻ News; Việt Nam News)

SDG 13: Climate Action

Most of us have heard about climate change, but knowing it exists is different from understanding how it connects to our lives. As Leonardo DiCaprio said, I believe humankind has looked at climate change in that same way: as if it were a fiction, happening to someone else's planet His words challenge the distance we often put between ourselves and the crisis. SDG 13 calls for action on climate change, and understanding its local impacts can help us see where to begin.

Look beyond the headlines: discover how climate change is affecting your community today. The future is one day away, so your action starts on day one.
